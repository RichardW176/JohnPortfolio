Filmmaker & Writer

John Recendez

I’m a Mexican-American and Indigenous Zapotec writer and filmmaker who moves between prose, poetry, screenwriting, and songwriting. My work mixes narrative, documentary, and lyric storytelling, shaped by the textures of Los Angeles and the myths and media I grew up with. Whether on the page or the screen, I’m interested in the connections that tug at us and those in our lives.
