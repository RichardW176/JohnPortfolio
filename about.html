Biography

About

I’m a Mexican-American and Indigenous Zapotec writer who moves between prose, poetry, screenwriting, and songwriting, primarily tracing the stories that grow out of Los Angeles and the people who inhabit it. My work blends lyricism, documentary sensibility, and mythic play, shaped by the languages, neighborhoods, and cultural inheritances that raised me. I’m drawn to the connections between people: the ways we influence, misunderstand, and remake experiences with one another across distance. I’m interested in projects that explore character, community, and place, and I’m always curious about new ways to tell those stories.
