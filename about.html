Biography

About

John Recendez is a filmmaker and writer based in Los Angeles. His short films have screened at regional festivals and his essays have appeared in print and online.

He came to film through writing — first scripts, then the discovery that some things only work when you can see the room they happen in. He studied at a university and has worked on productions of various sizes since.

He is currently developing a first feature and continues to write about film, memory, and the places people leave.
