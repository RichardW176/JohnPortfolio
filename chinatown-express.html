Spring 2024 · Short Film

Chinatown Express

When two best friends learn they have different ideas for the future, their ideals clash, and they talk it out.

Synopsis

Two childhood friends realize they can’t both go in the same direction.

This was my first short film project I worked on, and I was going for that “what could’ve been” ache of two close friends who have to go separate ways.
